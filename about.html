About

Hello! I'm Dhruv, a Developer Advocate at Voiden. I write short posts about things I find interesting — usually software, sometimes opinions, occasionally photos. Most of my posts are two or three paragraphs long because I think the best writing respects the reader's time.

As a Developer Advocate, I spend my time building things, breaking things, and helping other developers do the same. I care about good tools, clear documentation, and software that respects its users.

I started this blog in 2026. If something here resonates — or if you think I'm wrong — I'd love to hear from you.
